What we collect

When you fill our Facebook / Instagram enquiry form, we receive the details you submit: your name, phone number, email, city and your answers to the form questions.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you regarding custom Pine Script indicator development and support — including a WhatsApp message about your enquiry. We do not sell your data.

Our team answers your WhatsApp messages. To help the team write a reply, an AI service (Google Gemini, on Google Cloud) may read your messages, your form answers, your name and short notes from our phone calls with you, and suggest a draft. A team member checks every reply before it is sent. The AI service never sends messages to you. We do not share your phone number with it.

If we speak on the phone, our team's call may be recorded and summarised, so that we can follow up on your request.

WhatsApp messages

Messages are sent through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). You can reply "Stop" or choose "Not now" at any time and we will not message you again.

Storage and sharing

Your details are stored in our lead records on Google Cloud (Mumbai region) and in Google Sheets. Some steps still run in our automation tool (Pabbly Connect). Our team gets alerts about your enquiry in a private Telegram group. Your details are shared only with our own team handling your enquiry.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, email us at pacpl.tradingdesk@gmail.com with your phone number. We will delete it within 30 days.
